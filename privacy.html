This policy explains how Dirham handles information. The app is local-first and does not require an account for normal offline use. Information stored by the app By default, transactions, categories, notes, dates, currency, language, theme, and other app settings remain in the app's private storage on your device. Dirham does not send this local data to its publisher's servers, sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising. Cloud sync is optional and disabled by default. If you create an account, sign in, and enable sync, Firebase/Google may process your email address, account identifier, synced transaction records, and synchronization metadata such as update times and record identifiers. Custom categories are currently device-local and are not synced. Firebase processing is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Terms. The default release build is ad-free. If ads are enabled in a future release, Google AdMob and its partners may process advertising identifiers and technical information to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps. Dirham currently does not use publisher-controlled analytics or crash-reporting tools and does not sell personal data. Permissions SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW is used only for the optional Dirham quick-entry overlay. FOREGROUND_SERVICE and POST_NOTIFICATIONS support its visible foreground-service notification. INTERNET is used only for optional sync and, if enabled, ad loading. Dirham does not read content from other apps, messages, passwords, or files outside its own data. Sharing, retention, and deletion We do not sell or rent financial data. Data is shared with Firebase/Google only when you enable the relevant account and sync features, with AdMob only when ads are enabled, or when legally required. Local data remains on your device until you delete it or clear/uninstall the app. To request deletion of a cloud account and associated data, contact friendlyfri2@gmail.com from the account email. We may retain limited information when required by law or needed to resolve disputes or prevent abuse. Security and children We use reasonable safeguards, including Android private app storage and secure Firebase connections when sync is enabled. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Dirham is not directed to children below the applicable legal age, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
